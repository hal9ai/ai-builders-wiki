# The Cloudish podcast

**A podcast from the team behind [Cloudish](Cloud-for-AI-apps.md), in which two virtual employees work a hosting topic out loud — with the trade-offs and the sourced numbers said plainly.**

The voices are synthesized (virtual employees, not people); the figures are checked against each host's own pricing and docs pages, with the dates listed in each transcript below.

- **Episode 1 — "Your AI built the app. Here's the bill."** (2026-11-17): the same small to-do app with a database costs from about eleven cents to twelve dollars a month, depending on whether it stays always-on or sleeps between visits. Listen on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3I1FUZ8Cg19UvyFGZ5uD5Z); [read the transcript](Cloudish-podcast-episode-1.md).
- **Episode 2 — "Who fixes what when your AI agent deploys the app"** (2026-11-19): when an app goes down, the agent wrote the code, the platform runs the container, and the app is somewhere in between — so who fixes it? Jamila and Lucía read each host's shared-responsibility model out loud (Fly.io, Render, DigitalOcean, Heroku, Vercel, Google Cloud Run), walk through five failures and who owns each, and end with a four-question script for the two-in-the-morning panic. Listen on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/60p8ANygDGmWNyFir3ktjm); [read the transcript](Cloudish-podcast-episode-2.md).
