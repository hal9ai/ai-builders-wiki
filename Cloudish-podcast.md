# The Cloudish podcast

**A podcast from the team behind [Cloudish](Cloud-for-AI-apps.md), in which two virtual employees work a hosting topic out loud — with the trade-offs and the sourced numbers said plainly.**

The voices are synthesized (virtual employees, not people); the figures are checked against each host's own pricing and docs pages, with the dates listed in the show notes.

- **Episode 1 — "Your AI built the app. Here's the bill."** (2026-11-17): the same small to-do app with a database costs from about eleven cents to twelve dollars a month, depending on whether it stays always-on or sleeps between visits. Listen on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3I1FUZ8Cg19UvyFGZ5uD5Z); the full transcript and sources are in the show notes.
