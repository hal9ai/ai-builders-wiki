Data protection by vendor

This page compares what the main AI vendors do with your data, plan by plan, with every answer linked to the vendor's own page as checked on 2026-10-28. For what the questions mean and what to do about them, start with Data protection when you use AI.

This is not legal advice. It summarizes public vendor pages to help you ask the right questions. Policies change often, your contract may say something different, and laws such as the GDPR put obligations on you as the app maker. For a real decision, read the current terms and ask a lawyer or your data protection officer.

Details and exact wording, by vendor

Anthropic

  • Consumer training is a choice. The update gives "users the choice to allow their data to be used to improve Claude"; "You're always in control of this setting"; new users "can select your preference in the signup process" (news). The page does not say which way the switch starts.
  • Consumer retention. If you allow training, retention is extended "to five years"; otherwise the "30-day data retention period" applies (same page). For conversations flagged by safety systems: "We retain inputs and outputs for up to 2 years and trust and safety classification scores for up to 7 years" (help).
  • Commercial. "By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products" to train (help). For the API, "we automatically delete inputs and outputs on our backend within 30 days of receipt or generation", with exceptions such as ZDR agreements and the Files API (help).
  • Location. "Inference geo: Only "us" and "global" are available", and "Workspace geo: Only "us" is currently available" (data residency). Through Amazon Bedrock or Google Cloud, those platforms' own regional options apply instead.

OpenAI

  • API. "data sent to the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI models", unless "you explicitly opt in"; "abuse monitoring logs are generated for all API feature usage and retained for up to 30 days"; ZDR and modified abuse monitoring are "subject to prior approval by OpenAI" (Your data). Data residency regions include "Europe (EEA + Switzerland)"; some regions store data at rest only, without regional processing (same page).
  • ChatGPT consumer and business. OpenAI's help center refused automated access on 2026-10-28. As indexed by search, it says: "When you use our services for individuals (Free, Plus, Pro), we may use your content to train our models", with an opt-out in Data controls (help †). Check these pages yourself in a browser.
  • Court-ordered retention. OpenAI has published statements about a US court case that required it to keep some ChatGPT and API data longer than usual. We could not open those pages on 2026-10-28, so we do not summarize them here. If this matters for you, read OpenAI's statement † directly.

Google

  • Gemini app. "Google uses your activity to provide, develop, and improve its services (including training generative AI models)"; "To stop your future chats from being reviewed to improve Google services, turn off your Keep Activity setting"; "Temporary chats and chats you have when Keep Activity is off are retained with your account for 72 hours"; "Reviewed chats are retained for up to three years" (Gemini Apps Privacy Hub). Work and school accounts "may be subject to different data handling terms" (same page).
  • Gemini API. On unpaid services, Google "uses the content you submit … to provide, improve, and develop" its products and "human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your API input and output"; on paid services, "Google doesn't use your prompts … or responses to improve our products" (Gemini API terms). For abuse monitoring, "Google retains the following data for fifty-five (55) days" (usage policies). "To achieve zero data retention, customers must take specific actions or avoid specific features", and grounding with Google Search stores data for 30 days (ZDR).
  • Vertex AI (Google Cloud's AI platform, now presented as Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform). "Google won't use your data to train or fine-tune any AI/ML models without your prior permission or instruction" (data governance). The eu endpoint "strictly covers data residency within EU member states"; the global endpoint "may be processed in any Google Cloud location around the world" (data residency).

Microsoft (Azure OpenAI in Microsoft Foundry)

  • Prompts and completions "are NOT available to OpenAI" and "are NOT used to train any generative AI foundation models without your permission or instruction"; data is "processed within the customer-specified geography (unless you are using a Global or DataZone deployment type)" (data privacy).
  • Customers "who meet additional Limited Access eligibility criteria" can "apply to modify abuse monitoring" (abuse monitoring).

Mistral

  • Mistral's help pages now call its consumer chat app Vibe. "Input and output data are used by default to train our artificial intelligence models" on Vibe, while "Enterprise customers are opted out of training by default" (help). Opt-out toggles for Vibe and the API are separate (help).
  • API retention: "we keep your Input and Output for the period necessary to generate the Output and then for thirty (30) rolling days to monitor abuse (unless zero data retention is activated)" (privacy policy). ZDR is "available only with pay-as-you-go and only for stateless API calls", and Mistral may "at our discretion, approve or deny it" (help).
  • "By default, your data is hosted in the European Union" (help).

Meta

  • "Meta uses your interactions with AIs to improve AI at Meta" (Meta help). In the EU, interactions with Meta AI "will also be used to train and improve our models", with "a link to a form where people can object" (Meta newsroom).
  • Since December 2025, Meta says it will "use your interactions with AI at Meta to personalize the content and ads you see" (Meta newsroom).
  • Meta's generative AI privacy pages on facebook.com could not be loaded on 2026-10-28, so retention and location are not covered here.
  • Llama models are open-weight: if you run them yourself, Meta receives nothing.

DeepSeek

  • The app may "use Inputs and Outputs to provide, maintain, operate, develop or improve the Services", subject to de-identification, and you "can opt out by turning off "Improve the model for everyone"" (terms of use).
  • "we directly collect, process and store your Personal Data in People's Republic of China"; data is kept "for as long as you have an account" (privacy policy).
  • The API terms we found do not address training, retention or location (open platform terms). DeepSeek's models are also open-weight, and other providers serve them under their own terms.
