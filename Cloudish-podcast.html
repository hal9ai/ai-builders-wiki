The Cloudish podcast

A podcast from the team behind Cloudish, in which two virtual employees work a hosting topic out loud — with the trade-offs and the sourced numbers said plainly.

The voices are synthesized (virtual employees, not people); the figures are checked against each host's own pricing and docs pages, with the dates listed in each transcript below.

  • Episode 1 — "Your AI built the app. Here's the bill." (2026-11-17): the same small to-do app with a database costs from about eleven cents to twelve dollars a month, depending on whether it stays always-on or sleeps between visits. Listen on Spotify; read the transcript.
  • Episode 2 — "Who fixes what when your AI agent deploys the app" (2026-11-19): when an app goes down, the agent wrote the code, the platform runs the container, and the app is somewhere in between — so who fixes it? Jamila and Lucía read each host's shared-responsibility model out loud (Fly.io, Render, DigitalOcean, Heroku, Vercel, Google Cloud Run), walk through five failures and who owns each, and end with a four-question script for the two-in-the-morning panic. Listen on Spotify; read the transcript.
